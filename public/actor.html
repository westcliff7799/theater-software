How it reads a script
  • Speaker names in CAPITALS on their own line, or Name: at the start of a line. In Word files a bold name also works.
  • Stage directions in italics, in [brackets] or (parentheses), or lines starting Enter / Exit.
  • Headings such as ACT I or SCENE 2, and the title page.
  • Word files keep the most formatting, so they import best. PDFs and plain text rely on the layout alone.
