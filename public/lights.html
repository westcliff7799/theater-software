Select some channels, then type R G 1 Enter to save them as group 1, or R P 1 Enter to save their colours as palette 1. Click a group to select it (shift-click adds to the selection). Click a palette to colour the selected channels.

Faders

House lights, work lights, preshow… They sit outside the cues and add to them. Select channels, set their levels, then press Set on a fader.

In Blind, GO, Back, Master and Blackout still work on stage; everything you type only changes the cue. Channels whose cue changed in Blind get a dashed outline in Live: X Enter brings them up to date.

Level colours: red set by hand, blue this cue brought it up, green this cue took it down, grey carried on from an earlier cue, amber lifted by a fader. Esc or ? closes this.
